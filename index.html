    About the Event

    The TEP Scavenger Hunt is the brainchild of outgoing National Consul J.R. Benning, who, after years of meetings, budgets, bylaws, expansion plans, appeals, and other activities traditionally associated with responsible fraternity leadership, decided his legacy should instead involve grown men forming human pyramids, singing karaoke, body surfing, and posing like mermaids.

    With his term rapidly approaching its conclusion, J.R. recognized the strategic advantage of launching an event for which his successor will ultimately have to answer.

    The rules are simple: complete the challenges, document the evidence, collect the points, and try to maintain what little dignity you brought aboard.

    J.R. accepts full credit for the idea, limited responsibility for its execution, and absolutely no liability for the photographs.

    Consider it his parting gift to the Fraternity.
